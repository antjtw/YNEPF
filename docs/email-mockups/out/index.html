Entry confirmation to the lifter

Subject: You're in: NESC Open 2027
Preview line: Your entry for NESC Open 2027 on 27 February 2027 is confirmed and paid.

You're in, Sam.

Your entry for NESC Open 2027 on 27 February 2027 is confirmed. Thank you for entering.

Event: Full power
Equipment: Classic
Category: Men's, Junior
Weight class: 83 kg
Federation: Yorkshire & North East
University: Durham University
Discount: £20.00 off
Paid: £20.00

Under 18 on the day? A parent or guardian needs to be with you at the competition. We have your guardian's details already.

Refunds are available until entries close on Saturday 13 February 2027, 6:00 pm. After that, we cannot refund entries.

Need to change something, or pull out? Email us and the entries team will sort it. You cannot edit an entry yourself yet, so please get in touch before entries close.

Email us about your entry: competitions@ynepf.co.uk

BEFORE COMPETITION DAY

Please make sure you know the current IPF Technical Rules (https://www.powerlifting.sport/rules/codes/info/technical-rules). It is up to you to have read and understood them before you lift.

Please also check that your kit is on the IPF approved list (https://www.powerlifting.sport/rules/codes/info/approved-list). Any kit that is not compliant will be rejected, and referees will not make exceptions, so check it now rather than at weigh-in.

Not sure about something? Email us at info@ynepf.co.uk and we will help.

Your name, club and weight class are shown on the entries list and the lifting order. Your date of birth is never published. If anything is wrong, or you want us to change or delete what we hold about you, just reply to this email.

Thanks,
The YNEPF team

You are getting this email because you entered a YNEPF competition.
