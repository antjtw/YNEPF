Please make sure you know the current IPF Technical Rules (https://www.powerlifting.sport/rules/codes/info/technical-rules). It is up to you to have read and understood them before you lift.

Please also check that your kit is on the IPF approved list (https://www.powerlifting.sport/rules/codes/info/approved-list). Any kit that is not compliant will be rejected, and referees will not make exceptions, so check it now rather than at weigh-in.
